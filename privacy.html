Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 May 2026

Information SiteMate uses

SiteMate stores the account, customer, job, invoice, expense, note, photo and receipt information you choose to add. The app can use microphone and speech recognition permissions when you choose to speak a day entry.

How information is used

Your information is used to provide app features such as sign in, cloud sync, job logging, invoice records, support, subscription status and AI-assisted day entry sorting.

Third-party services

SiteMate uses Supabase for account and cloud data services, Apple for Sign in with Apple and subscriptions, and OpenAI-backed processing through secure backend functions for AI day entry features.

Your choices

You can delete your SiteMate account in the app. You can also contact support@sitemate.app for help with privacy questions.
